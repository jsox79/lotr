# MOVEMENT_CANON

_Status: CURRENT / LOCKED DIRECTION_
_Date: 2026-09-14_

## Core Rule

Movement follows the Greater Region adjacency graph.

Entering or passing through another player's region does **not** automatically initiate combat.

Presence, control, passage, and hostility are separate game concepts.

A player may pass through or occupy a region containing another player's forces unless a specific rule, Enemy, Event, Location, or initiated attack says otherwise.

## Boundary Trigger

Whenever a Hero or traveling stack crosses from one Greater Region into another, draw and resolve one Movement card from the destination region's unique Movement Deck.

This applies when:

- entering a region as the final destination;
- passing through a region during multi-region movement.

Do not draw a Movement card merely because a turn begins in a region or because a player remains stationary.

Leaving and later re-entering a region triggers another draw.

## Regional Movement Decks

Each Greater Region has its own persistent shuffled Movement Deck.

These decks are separate from the Universal Deck.

Movement-card families include:

- Discovery
- Skirmish
- Delay
- Speed Boon
- Retreat
- Hazard
- Encounter
- Quiet Passage
- Betrayal

Each region's deck should reflect the identity, danger, people, terrain, Corruption, and stories of that region.

Regional control and Corruption may modify how a Movement card resolves.

## Skirmish vs Battle

A **Skirmish** is a travel/world encounter, usually generated by a Movement card.

A **Battle** is conflict involving established forces or stacks.

A **Player Attack / Challenge** is a deliberate decision to initiate conflict against another player's force or claim.

Movement alone does not create a Battle.

## Retreat

A Retreat Movement card sends the traveling stack back to the previous Greater Region and ends that movement unless the card specifically says otherwise.

The intent is regrouping, not destruction.

## Secret Stair

**Secret Stair** is a rare Mordor Travel Boon.

When discovered, retain it.

Once per game, discard Secret Stair to make one free movement from a region adjacent to Mordor into Mordor.

That movement:

- costs no normal movement;
- bypasses the normal Mordor entry cost or restriction;
- does not draw a Mordor Movement card for that specific entry.

It does not bypass forces, Enemies, control, or consequences already present inside Mordor.

Secret Stair is a Travel Boon, not an Artifact.

## Crumbs on His Jacketses

**Crumbs on His Jacketses** is a Mordor Betrayal Movement card.

Current direction:

- choose one Bannerman / Supporting Hero or Ally traveling with the Active Hero;
- separate that character from the traveling stack and return / leave them in the previous region;
- end the Active Hero's movement so the company must regroup.

Exact numerical or Corruption interactions remain subject to balance testing.
