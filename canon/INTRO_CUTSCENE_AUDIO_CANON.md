# INTRO / CUTSCENE / AUDIO LANGUAGE — CANON

_Status: CURRENT CANON_
_Date: 2026-09-14_

## Canonical Title Composition

The original metal instrumental **My Precious** is the canonical opening/title composition for the game.

The opening cinematic should be authored against the actual music timeline rather than treating the track as generic background music. Major visual changes, lightning, reveals, cuts, fades and the title payoff should synchronize with musically meaningful attacks, transitions, builds and releases.

The full composition may play as an extended title/attract sequence, but the intro must be skippable immediately so repeat players are never forced to wait through the entire track.

---

## Opening Image

The opening begins in near-total darkness.

The central visual thread is the **One Ring**, shown as though recently heated in fire:

- gold metal emerging from darkness;
- inscription becoming visibly incandescent;
- heat shimmer, embers, smoke and subtle reflected firelight;
- surrounding darkness capable of falling nearly black;
- HDR10 presentation may use the Ring inscription and fire as strong highlight elements.

The Ring should remain the visual object to which the cinematic repeatedly returns.

---

## Darkness, Lightning and Mount Doom

The background presentation is storm-dark and atmospheric rather than constantly busy.

Lightning should be used sparingly and intentionally. Brief flashes may expose imagery for fractions of a second before returning to darkness, including:

- Mount Doom;
- Mordor;
- Nazgûl or other Shadow silhouettes;
- armies or threats;
- storm terrain and distant structures.

Lightning and other hard visual attacks should be synchronized to appropriate musical transients rather than fired randomly during the authored intro.

---

## Light / Character Flashes

The darkness is interrupted by short Light-side scenes and character appearances.

Characters or locations may emerge for roughly one or two seconds, then dissolve/fade back into darkness. Examples include Gandalf, Aragorn, Galadriel, Frodo, Rohan, Gondor, Lothlórien and other canonical Heroes/regions.

These should not feel like cards being displayed. They are cinematic glimpses into Middle-earth.

Light imagery should temporarily change the emotional temperature of the entire screen rather than simply brightening a portrait. Examples:

- Galadriel/Lothlórien may transform the frame toward luminous white and gold;
- Rohan may reveal wind, grass and open light;
- Gondor may emerge in pale stone and sunrise-like illumination.

The scene then yields again to darkness and Shadow.

---

## Escalation

As **My Precious** builds, visual edits may become shorter and more aggressive while remaining painterly and atmospheric rather than becoming a rapid generic action trailer.

Possible escalating imagery includes:

- Heroes;
- armies;
- Enemies;
- Rohan and Gondor;
- Nazgûl;
- Isengard;
- corrupted Hero imagery;
- Balrog or legendary threats;
- Mordor;
- Mount Doom;
- the Ring and its inscription.

The climax should increasingly collapse the imagery back toward the Ring so that the sequence communicates that the conflict ultimately revolves around it.

---

## Title Payoff

Near the musical climax/end, darkness should briefly reclaim the frame before a major synchronized musical hit reveals the Ring/title presentation.

Preferred effect:

- frame falls nearly black;
- major musical attack lands;
- Ring inscription ignites/punches through the darkness;
- title resolves;
- final musical tail transitions naturally into the interactive start screen.

Avoid a generic explosion. The impact should come from contrast, luminance, music and the sudden clarity of the Ring.

---

## Start Screen

The cinematic resolves into a **live start/title scene**, not a dead static image.

The start screen should retain the One Ring and Mount Doom/darkness visual identity, with subtle continuous life such as:

- drifting smoke;
- embers;
- heat shimmer;
- subtle Ring motion or light movement;
- distant atmospheric movement;
- occasional restrained lightning;
- distant Mount Doom/fire activity.

The screen should provide a clear Begin/Continue interaction while preserving cinematic atmosphere.

After the full composition ends, the title screen may transition to quieter looping ambience or a purpose-built menu loop derived from the musical identity of **My Precious**.

---

## Cutscene Engine

The TV renderer should include a lightweight **real-time 2.5D cinematic compositor** rather than relying exclusively on prerendered video.

The system may combine:

- layered artwork;
- parallax/depth planes;
- camera pans, pushes and pulls;
- fades and masks;
- particles;
- smoke, fog, rain, embers and environmental layers;
- lighting and grading;
- subtle character/environment motion;
- audio cues;
- music-timeline cues;
- timed transitions;
- SDR/HDR10 presentation profiles.

This allows cinematic presentation to reuse the game's server-delivered art assets and remain lightweight on the installed TV client.

---

## Gameplay Cutscenes

The same compositor should support short in-engine cinematic moments for major game events rather than requiring full prerecorded movies.

Strong candidates include:

- the One Ring being discovered;
- a Hero choosing to Embrace the Shadow;
- a Hero reaching a final corrupted form;
- a Legendary Enemy appearing;
- the Balrog appearing;
- first entry into Mordor;
- a Greater Region becoming fully corrupted;
- major faction/world-state changes;
- Saruman's fall or equivalent major character events;
- arrival at Mount Doom;
- destruction/loss of the Ring;
- major victory-condition endings.

These sequences should be concise enough not to interrupt tabletop pacing and should normally remain skippable where appropriate.

---

## Presentation Philosophy

The intro is not merely decoration. Without explaining rules, it should establish the game's core emotional conflict:

**Light → Shadow → Heroes → armies → corruption → Mordor → the Ring.**

The visual and musical language should communicate that Middle-earth is being contested and that the Ring remains the dangerous object around which everything turns.

### North Stars

**My Precious is composed into the opening; it is not simply played underneath it.**

**The Ring is the visual thread connecting the entire title sequence.**

**Cutscenes should feel alive through layered real-time presentation rather than requiring heavyweight video files.**

**Light and Shadow should be felt before they are explained.**
