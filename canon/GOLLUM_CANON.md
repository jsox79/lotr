# GOLLUM_CANON

_Status: CURRENT / LOCKED DIRECTION_
_Date: 2026-09-14_

## Identity

Gollum is random disruption.

He is not an Ally, Bannerman, companion asset, retained Scheme, strategic resource, or player-controlled tool.

His job is to make possession of the One Ring dangerous and unpredictable.

## Card

**Gollum**
Type: Special / Unique Disruption
Copies: 3 in the Universal Deck unless later balance tuning changes the count.

When Gollum is drawn, reveal and resolve him immediately.

### If the One Ring is currently possessed

If the One Ring is in any player's hand or attached to any Hero:

- Gollum takes it;
- remove the One Ring from that player / Hero;
- shuffle the One Ring back into the Universal Deck;
- this effect cannot be prevented, redirected, stored, targeted, or strategically delayed.

Then discard Gollum.

### If nobody possesses the One Ring

Gollum has no mechanical effect.

The game may present a brief humorous Gollum interruption / complaint, then discard him immediately.

## Design Intent

Gollum is a random catastrophe tied to Ring possession.

Players should not be able to save him, aim him at another player, build around him as an asset, or recruit him for travel advantages.

The One Ring may enable powerful victory strategies, but it is never completely safe while Gollum remains possible in the Universal Deck.

END OF GOLLUM_CANON
